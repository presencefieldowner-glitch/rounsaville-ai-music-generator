"Play Live" doesn't play the WAV above — it synthesizes the composition (notes, chords, timing) directly in your browser via the Web Audio API, in real time, at the moment you click play. The audio genuinely isn't fixed until then. Its pluck timbre is an approximation (a fast-decaying oscillator) of the server's real Karplus-Strong physical modeling, since that needs an AudioWorklet this page doesn't include.

Lyrics

Real, algorithmically generated lyrics — template-filled lines drawn from mood-themed word banks, with true rhymes (grouped by actual sound, not spelling) closing every second line. This is not an LLM. These words are never sung by the generated audio: the "vocal" track is a wordless, pitch-matched melody line (see 003_AUDIO_ENGINE), since this system has no text-to-singing-voice synthesis. The phrases below (under Voice Profile) are drawn directly from these lyrics so recording them measures your pitch on the actual generated words — but it still does not make the output audio pronounce them, and it does not clone your voice's timbre.
